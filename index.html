Deadlines are in European time; Pakistan is ahead
Many Erasmus Mundus portals close at a stated hour in CET (for example 13:00 CET on 10 Jan 2027 for one 2027 programme). In winter Pakistan time is 4 hours ahead of CET, so that is 17:00 in Lahore. Submit at least a day early; portals slow down near closing.

Policy change
US ended “duration of status” for F-1 students
A DHS final rule replaces open-ended stay with fixed admission periods, up to four years for most F and J visa holders. Longer stays need USCIS approval, the post-study departure grace period falls from 60 to 30 days, and programme changes face tighter limits. Reported as effective 15 September 2026.
